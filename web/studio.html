Getting the whole room

A phone's built-in Panorama mode usually gives up somewhere around 200–270°, so one sweep often leaves part of the room out. The app measures what you actually captured and fences the viewer to it, but the missing wall is still missing. Three ways round it, best first:

  1. Use a dedicated 360° photo app. Google Street View (free, iOS and Android) walks you through a grid of dots and produces a true photo sphere — full 360×180 with exact geometry embedded. This app reads that metadata and uses the image as-is, no conversion. Best possible result.
  2. Sweep further. Start facing a corner rather than a wall, turn smoothly, and keep going until the phone stops on its own. Rushing makes it stop early.
  3. Accept the partial view. For a small room a 240° sweep of the interesting side is often enough for a listing. Aim the middle of the sweep at whatever matters most.
