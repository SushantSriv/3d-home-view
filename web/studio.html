Turn a viewing into one link

Photograph each room with your phone, drop a pin for it on the floor plan, and you get a single public web address. Paste it into the listing. Buyers open it with no login and no app, walking the home room by room.

What to have ready

  • A floor plan image — a screenshot of the listing plan is fine.
  • One panorama photo per room, from your phone's Panorama mode.
  • About ten minutes.

  1. Take the photos

    Stand in the middle of the room and sweep. A partial sweep is allowed.

  2. Place them on the plan

    Click the plan where each room is. Drag a pin to move it.

  3. Share the link

    Publish, copy, paste into the listing. Nothing to install.

Getting the whole room
  1. A photo sphere from the Google Street View app is read exactly as-is — it carries the true angles in its metadata, so nothing is inferred.
  2. Panorama mode usually gives up somewhere around 200–270°. Pick two overlapping sweeps at once and they are joined, but overlap them by about a third or the join has nothing to lock on to.
  3. A partial view is allowed. The app measures what you actually captured and stops the viewer at the edges rather than inventing the rest.
