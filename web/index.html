How it works

  1. Capture — open the studio on your phone, press Capture 360°, and turn slowly on the spot. The phone's motion sensors tell the app where each frame belongs, so a strip along the top fills in as you go and you can see what is still missing. Hold the phone upright and turn about your own axis rather than swinging it at arm's length.
    Already have a panorama photo? Upload that instead.
  2. Upload — drop in the clips plus a floor plan image.
  3. Pin — click the floor plan where each room is, name it, add the size in m² if you know it.
  4. Stitch — each clip becomes an equirectangular panorama. This runs offline, not in your browser.
  5. Share — publish, copy the link, paste it into the listing.

Built on Pannellum and Kronbii/360-spherical-stitching, both MIT licensed. Not a continuous 3D scan — a set of linked 360° panoramas, one per room.
