Three steps

  1. Photograph

    Stand in the middle of the room, open your phone's Panorama mode and turn slowly on the spot. A part-circle is fine — most phones stop near 230°, and the tour is built to show exactly as much as you captured.

  2. Place on the plan

    Upload the floor plan, then click the spot where you stood in each room. Give it a name and the size in m². That plan becomes the buyer's map, and the pins become the doors they walk through.

  3. Share one link

    Publish, copy the link, paste it into the listing. One address covers the whole home, it opens straight in the buyer's browser, and you can keep editing behind it.
